    Tech In School · TISPRO

    TISPRO, powered by Oy-da.

    • 1

      Consistent, pre-recorded lessons

      Lessons are carefully prepared and recorded, so every participating school gets the same standard of learning.

    • 2

      Never a passive lesson

      Short sessions run Video → Quiz → Video → Activity → Quiz → Review, keeping students involved throughout.

    • 3

      A tutor in every classroom

      Our tutor supports students, fixes technical issues, helps with activities and questions, and keeps focus.

    Oy-da: the app behind the classroom

    Oy-da is being developed to power the TISPRO learning and classroom system. It brings every part into one structured platform, making lessons more interactive and class management lighter.

    • Time & Class Management
    • Attendance
    • Pre-recorded Lessons
    • Interactive Quizzes & Activities
    • Digital Notes
    • Assignments
    • CBT & Examinations
    • Student Progress Tracking
    • Automated Reports

    Coming as TISPRO grows: through partnerships with specialised ed-tech providers, what students learn in class can become interactive challenges and educational games. This experience is separate from Oy-da and will come via an external technology partner.
